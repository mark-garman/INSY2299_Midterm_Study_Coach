How this works

Write each formula on paper first, then type what you wrote. The coach does not compare text. It tests your formula with the numbers shown, with changed inputs, with a loss year, and after copying it right or down. Many different formulas are correct. Every new problem uses new numbers. Each answer counts toward the skills it uses, and My skills shows where to practice next. Your results stay in this browser only.
